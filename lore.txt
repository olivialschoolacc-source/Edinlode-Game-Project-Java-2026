CELENODE {
    LOCATION: northwest of Edinlode,
    PEOPLE: people are called Celenish,
    CULTURE: [
        has very skilled sorcery with cold magic. Celenish people all know the basics of cold magic and experts
        even go as far to teach in the best cold magic universities of Edinlode. The most popular case is the Frigidus
        College, where all the professors of the school are from Celenode. There are slight rumors that the school
        does not accept to hire non-Celenish people in the first place, which is likely false, considering it is very
        true that no other region beats Celenish people in the art of cold magic.
        ]
    AREAS: [
        Djila Tower: {
            in the furthermost corner in the southwest of Celenode is a small pocket of space surrounded by
            tall icy mountain peaks titled Djila. In the center lies the Djila Tower, a tower habited by the past generations
            of cold magic masters with the title of witches and wizards. These witches and wizards, although being masters
            of cold magic, do not move to teach in schools, and instead stays in Celenode to research and continue the line
            of blood of the witches and wizards. These masters have access to the top secret magic spells for those officially
            titled witches and wizards.
        }
        Equix Nawflots: {
            Just a slight bit north of the Great Mountains of Plia is the capital city of Celenode. In this city is the castle
            of the generational and historical royals' bloodline. This city is the most alive of all cities, and is the center
            of all development that happens in Celenode. The downside to this is that travelling to the capital from other
            towns and cities is of overpriced transportations, and because most commoners only know the basic daily life magic,
            they cannot fight against the monsters that block the direct paths to the capital. This only leaves the choice to
            dream about reaching the capital one day for those who were not born there.
        }
        Klinde Bay: {
            A bit to the northeast of the capital lies a big town with the name Klinde Bay. Here, the transportational dragons
            to the floating island takes flight. The people of Klinde bay are dragon riding masters who prioritizes mastering
            cold magic purely for controlling the ice dragons of Celenode. Yet the unfortunate part of these people are that
            these Celenish dragons are legally not permitted to leave certain areas for safety concerns after a dragon nearly
            broke down the castle in the capital. And the dragons in Klinde Bay are not trained to fight, therefore these
            dragons cannot be used for battle and only for transportation.
        }
        The Floating Island: {
            This island does not have a proper name. It is called the Floating Islands for the simple reason that it is an
            island that floats. Anybody can really go up there as long as they have enough money to pay for the dragon ride
            at Klinde Bay. Above the island is one big castle-like building which is a university that consists of three
            different sectors. One for dragon flying, one for cold magic mastery, and one for recordkeeping and scribe
            training.
        }
        Cfella: {
            The only other area in Celenode where a dragon is legally permitted. The Cfella House is the main control unit
            of this town, and is the only house that owns a dragon breeding farm. From here, all of the dragons are born
            and sold to Klinde Bay and other dragon riding individuals.
        }
        Orvidam: {
            A small refugee camp which is also under control of the Cfella House.
        }
        Prostuu Harbor: {
            The only harbor of Celenode which is responsible for the import and exporting of goods from and to other regions.
            This city is owned by the Prostuu House, who have strong lineage of scribes. The people of the Prostuu house
            prioritize scribe education over any other, and mainly focus on further sea research and trades across water.
            This city is the second largest city of Celenode after the capital, and is also a big goal for those who wish for
            a cityscape from the towns.
        }
    ]
    CURRENT ISSUES: [
        More Harsh Weathers: {
            As the cold gets more severe over time, gathering resources and researching gets difficult to pursue. This may be
            because the magical orb created by Frosten 600 years ago is started to fade away in power...
        }
        Enemies Start Losing Sanity: {
            Another effect of the harsh weathers is the enemies that guard the paths to and from towns and cities are starting
            to go insane. They start becoming overpowered and more uncontrollable over time. This makes travelling safely among
            areas very dangerous and more expensive.
        }
        Dark Magic Wielder on Loose: {
            There is a magic wielder with a cold magic mastery on the loose. This wizard is using the magic for evil purposes,
            and is the highest possibility to the weakening of Frosten's sealing orb.
        }
    ]
    CHARACTERS: [
        Karsen Celen III: the king of Celenode
        Mira Celen: the queen of Celenode
        Jude Celen: the crowned heir of Celenode
        Krys Provit: the dark magic wielder on loose
        FURTHER UPDATES AVAILABLE
    ]
}

FELENA: {
    LOCATION: to the south of Celenode
    PEOPLE: people ara called Felenian
    CULTURE: [
        Very strong suit in nature. As they live in trees, they have adapted to all sorts of dangers natural happenings give,
        and they are very reliable when it comes to the knowledge about plants. Their high knowledge for plants give them a
        dangerous trait: mastery in poison. They have a wide range of land and have great accesibility to other regions,
        helping their resource collection and research based on the poison and toxin making techniques. Though because their
        land is so big and there are plenty of towns and villages in unfortunate states, only the rich have the chance to
        be educated with these poison alchemy techniques.
    ]
    AREAS: [
        Onixya: {
            Above the Merchi Lake is a popular university campus that mainly focuses on alchemy for poison making and poison
            curing, as well as informations about toxic plants and often times healing as well are offered. This university
            is the most reccomended campus out of all others if you are willing to learn poison alchemy in the lands of poison.
        }
        Pondokarrad: {
            A small town that consists of tall trees which all have treehouses mounted on them. Being right below the Great
            Mountains of Plia, this town is a slight bit chillier than the other areas and offer a great environment to grow
            plants that can only be found in cold regions.
        }
        Jerstad: {
            A lonely tree standing beside the Merchi Lake which belongs to the Jersti House. The Jersti House does not focus
            their education on poison, but rather healing. They research about healing bodies rather than harming them, and all
            professors under the healing department in Onixya are either people of the Jersti House or rich individuals who paid
            to be educated by the direct sources themselves.
        }
        Minnditad: {
            An expansion of the Jersti House's research units. This is a small camp that has started to become used to a town
            system which brings technology over from Onixya and Jerstad to pursue research in the river.
        }
        Foilo: {
            A giant tree stump which has a whole entire underground town. This town focuses on food, having the best chefs of
            Edinlode and a popular street food vending alley with many vendors and diverse food, even from different regions as
            well, making it a cheaper alternative for those who wish to experience a new region's cuisine without needed to cross
            rivers and seas.
        }
        May Hays: {
            The capital city of Felena, Merchi. Located to the east of the Merchi Lake, which was named after the castle. May Hays
            is also a center point for all sorts of systems. Importing and Exporting to other regions is done at the harbor that
            faces Jestemex, and all the lower-ranked university and educational institudes are also collectively located in
            May Hays.
        }
        Lestergat: {
            Another lonely tree takes place just a slight bit south of May Hays, owned by the Lestergat Family. This family is an
            expansion of the royal family, but the only reason why they are split is because they had different educational values.
            The Lestergat family valued diverse scribe education and recordkeeping which contrasted the violent and protective
            nature of the royal family, who needed more destructive power of the poison alchemy educations for battle. There is no
            cracks in the relationship among them and they are not foes of each other, but the split gives an effective system for
            research and resource collection, which happens in Lestergat, and these information gets sold or transported to other
            areas of Felena.
        }
        Piancho: {
            A research camp expansion from the Lestergat Family.
        }
        Yul Shak: {
            A vast jungle of tall trees and treehouses. This town is quite unique, as it is run by a mafia. The Yul Family has been
            a strong mafia force since centuries before, and only recently did they earn the trust to take control over Yul Shak.
            So far, the control is going smoothly, as the Yul Family's only uses of violence has always been to enemy mafias and
            not commoners. The town has actually reduced in crime and violence since the mafia took over, and is showing a clear
            positive impact.
        }
        Klimindit: {
            A giant city under one of the two largest boulders in Felena. Felenians who live under this rock has adapted to the
            darkness and have developed quality technology of resource collection in mines and more rocky environments that
            contrast the soft nature environments.
        }
        Posodat: {
            The most dangerous area in all of Felena. Maybe even Edinlode if we do not count the deadlands. This giant lake is
            filled with pure toxic poison that can easily kill a man with less than a minute of exposion to the gas it makes.
            A famous issue was when the serial killer that roamed around Daster back 200 years ago was rumored to have thrown
            away the bodies of the victims into this acid pool and it removed the evidence entirely.
        }
        Daster: {
            This town is the land of the criminals and the jungle of crime. Being the town farthest away from any royal control,
            anybody from Daster automatically gets a stereotypical eye of being a criminal, which is also likely true, as 97%
            of all of Felena and Kiandle's mafia crew start off in Daster. This town has been such a disgrace to the image of
            Felena, that 50 years back, the crown decided to remove its locational marking entirely from the map.
        }
        Kii: {
            A forest town owned by the Kii House. Simply just another town, nothing quite special except for the fact that the
            Felenians in Kii have a free access trip to the giant sword in the middle of the Merchi Lake every year if you are
            underage and a student.
        }
        Iquonder: {
            A city owned by the two most wealthiest houses: the Iquond House and the Kii House. The Iquond house has the full
            control and ownership over the dragon breeding farm and flight related suppplies as well as the Clestian Fields for
            flight practicing. Meanwhile, the Kii House has full control and ownership over the giant sword in the center of the
            Merchi Lake and the forest town of Kii.
        }
        The Giant Sword: {
            A gift to the Kii house's duke from 800 years back which was given from the duke's lover who was Jestemexi. The
            transportation fee to get to the center of the Merchi Lake is pricy but the people of Kii and Iquonder get free
            trips every year. Legends say touching the sword gives a healthy and safe environment for up to a year.
        }
    ]
    CURRENT ISSUES: [
        Mafia War: {
            Recently, a strong mafia force from Daster has challenged a mafia force in Kiandle, and is about to break into war
            with them. This war could greatly disturb the relationship between the two regions and trade routes to the regions
            around it.
        }
        Professor Eliminations: {
            A illegal gangster group from Daster has started kidnapping and killing off the valued professors of an unpopular
            college campus. A letter to the crown says their next target is Onixya, but currently there is too much to lose...
        }
        Posodat Cleansing: {
            The acid pool in Posodat has truely intrigued many researchers of Felena. If there was a safe way to retrieve the
            materials from Posodat and maybe even cleanse the dangerous poison pool...
        }
    ]
    CHARACTERS: [
        Chauve Felen: the king of Felena
        Paula Felen: the queen of Felena
        Vars Felen: the crowned prince of Felena
        Nicky Lestergat: the duchess of the Lestergat Family
        Yaf Yul: the mafia boss of the Yul Family
        Dennis Osof: the mafia boss of the Osof Family (the mafia attacking Kiandle)
        Notra Puuvba: headmaster of Onixya
        FURTHER UPDATES AVAILABLE
    ]
}
